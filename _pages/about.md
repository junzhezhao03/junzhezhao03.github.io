I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Operations Management at the School of Management, University of Science and Technology of China, advised by [Lindong Liu](https://sites.google.com/view/ldliu/home).

My primary research interest is in sustainable operations. I use analytical models to study how voluntary initiatives (e.g., carbon offsetting) and mandatory policies (e.g., ReFuelEU Aviation) shape firms’ decisions and emissions across supply chains.

Another stream of my research focuses on cooperative game theory. I am particularly interested in characterizing core nonemptiness and developing efficient methods for computing core allocations.
